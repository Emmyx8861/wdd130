Our River Trips

Ready to get wet? Explore our options below and Contact Us to book your spot!

The Family Floater

A gentle Class I-II introduction to the river. Safe for ages 5 and up. Perfect for a relaxing afternoon outdoors.

Canyon Charger

Exciting Class III rapids through deep rock canyons. This full-day trip includes a riverside lunch and great views.

Adrenaline Peak

Challenge yourself with Class IV-V rapids. This trip is physically demanding and perfect for experienced thrill-seekers.
